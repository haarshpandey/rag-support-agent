# Acme Cloud — Billing & Refunds

## Refund policy

Refunds are issued for **unused time** on annual plans only. Monthly plans are
non-refundable after the first 7 days.

To request a refund:
1. Open Settings > Billing in the dashboard.
2. Click **Request refund** and select the invoice.
3. Refunds are processed within **5–10 business days** to the original payment method.

## Changing plans

You can upgrade at any time; the new plan is prorated immediately. Downgrades
take effect at the **next billing cycle** and are not prorated.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, the account enters a **7-day grace period**. During the
grace period all features remain active. After 7 days without a successful
payment, the workspace is suspended (data is retained for 90 days).
