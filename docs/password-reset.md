# Acme Cloud — Password Reset

## How to reset your password

1. Go to the login page and click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
3. You will receive a reset link valid for **30 minutes**. The link can only be used once.
4. Choose a new password with at least 12 characters, including one number and one symbol.

If you do not receive the email within 10 minutes, check your spam folder, then contact support.

## Locked accounts

After **5 failed login attempts**, an account is locked for 15 minutes.
Administrators can unlock accounts early from the admin console under
Users > Security > Unlock.
